# OCA/l10n-usa


> **OCA Community Modules (18.0)**
> Source: https://github.com/OCA/l10n-usa/tree/18.0

## Purpose

OCA US localization: state-level tax configuration, US chart of accounts adjustments, and American date/address formats. Use for US-based Odoo deployments.
